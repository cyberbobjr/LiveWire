# Recipes

The 17 Live Wire recipes are in the crafting menu. Electrical recipes unlock automatically at their required level; the blade set and mast are in **Metalworking** and need no Electrical level. Tools are kept (some can wear). Every recipe awards Electrical XP, including blades, masts and tester dismantling.

## Wind turbine parts and amplifiers

Make a blade set with two metal sheets and two screws (metal snips and hammer),
and a mast with one metal pipe, two metal bars and two screws (hammer and pipe wrench).
Neither part requires an Electrical level. Assemble both parts with two amplifiers,
three wires, four electronics scrap and two screws at Electrical 6. The water wheel
still requires Electrical 7 to craft, also using two amplifiers.

Blade sets and masts can also be found in technical loot. Amplifiers are vanilla
parts recovered by dismantling devices. The vanilla speaker recipe yields one
amplifier and one electronics scrap. Their loot rates are unchanged. Both complete
machines require only Electrical 2 to place or pick up. Existing machines and IDs
remain valid. The two new parts currently use vanilla placeholder icons and models.

Equipment recipes show their output icon after a full game restart.

## Dismantle a voltage tester

The crafting recipe **Dismantle Voltage Tester** consumes one Live Wire voltage
tester and gives one electronics scrap. A working screwdriver is required and
kept. No Electrical level is required; the action takes 60 recipe-time units
and awards 2 Electrical XP. Wire, bulb and battery used in its construction are
not recovered, so a craft/dismantle cycle cannot create materials.

Every Live Wire recipe awards Electrical XP; blades and masts each award 2 XP.
