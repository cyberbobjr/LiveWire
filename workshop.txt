description=[h1]Live Wire - Electrical Skill Overhaul[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer (server-authoritative)[/b]
description=
description=The power is out for good... but the lights do not have to be. Build your own off-grid electricity: solar panels, a wind turbine or a water wheel feed an inverter, and the inverter powers the whole house around it. Add batteries for the night. The Electrical skill finally matters: every device has a level, and your level makes the installation better.
description=
description=[h2]1.6.0 — Control your installation[/h2]
description=
description=The electrical panel has lighting, equipment and main levers, plus instant circuit/total watts. Cut real appliances and restore their previous choices, including after a restart. Sources can keep charging while main distribution is open. Left-click each device for its LCD; use Connections to choose its inverter.

description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/images/diagrams/diagram_en.png[/img]
description=
description=Solar 100 W, wind 400 W, water wheel 120 W. An inverter supplies appliances within 20 tiles and ±3 floors, up to 1,500 W; couple four for 6,000 W. No cable per household appliance.
description=Store surplus in power banks (4 batteries each). Cables: 1 wire per 4 tiles, range 12. Choose Game values, Realistic 1993, Ultra-realistic 1993 or Comfort consumption in sandbox options.
description=
description=[h2]Equipment[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/images/diagrams/equipment_en.png[/img]
description=
description=Place solar panels outdoors (Electrical 3), turbines outdoors and water wheels on river banks (both 2), inverters (5) and power banks (2). Stack two banks on one tile, with separate inventories and connections; empty them before removal, upper bank first.
description=Optional: breaker, battery alarm, soundproofing, vanilla backup generator and management computer.
description=
description=[h2]Production[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/images/diagrams/production_en.png[/img]
description=
description=Solar direction matters: south 100%, east/west 85%, north 55%. Clouds, snow and dirt reduce output; rain washes panels, or clean with a rag + 0.5 L water. Wind produces from 10 km/h, reaches full power at 45–85 and brakes above. The water wheel produces day and night. Optional battery cold effects retain unavailable energy as a reserve; off by default.
description=
description=[h2]Water pump[/h2]
description=
description=A water pump (Electrical 6) supplies plumbed fixtures within 15 tiles from up to 8 adjacent rain collectors or [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3436537035]Useful Barrels[/url] barrels, one metal pipe each. Use a pipe wrench for plumbing. Rainwater comes out clean: up to 5 L/min, 250 W while pumping. Blue light = powered, even idle. Before uninstalling, remove all water relays with the debug/admin action.
description=
description=[h2]Recipes[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/images/diagrams/recipe_book_en.png[/img]
description=
description=17 recipes, illustrated below. Electrical recipes unlock automatically; blades and masts are in Metalworking with no Electrical gate. Assemble a turbine at level 6; craft a water wheel at 7, both using two amplifiers. Place/pick up either at 2. Dismantle a voltage tester for scrap. Every recipe grants Electrical XP. Broader equipment/parts loot; sandbox multiplier 5 by default (1–20), future rolls only after restart.
description=
description=[h2]Management software[/h2]
description=
description=Live Wire Monitor adds consumers (up to 200), coloured appliance silhouettes, cable views, the last 30 events and a three-day production forecast with a powered management computer, alongside 48 h history. Network names appear on LCDs and alerts. Vanilla desktops work; optional Computer Mod/Laptop Mod use a loot disk.

description=[h2]Electrical safety and XP[/h2]
description=
description=Test batteries at Electrical 1/3/5 for more detail. Recondition at 4 (6 if dead): kept screwdriver, scrap, wire, 0.5 L water; +15 condition, max 80, four times. Salvage dead empty equipment at 3. Live-work risk is on by default: 50/42/33/25/17/8/0% at levels 0/1/2/3/4/5/6+. A shock burns the tool hand, drops held items and aborts before spending materials. Switch off the master inverter; opening the breaker does not isolate it.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=
description=Server-authoritative simulation and action checks, saved circuit choices and appliance state synchronisation. Close both circuits before disabling the mod.
