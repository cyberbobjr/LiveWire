description=[h1]Live Wire - Electrical Skill Overhaul[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21 · Singleplayer & Multiplayer (server-authoritative)[/b]
description=
description=The power is out for good... but the lights do not have to be. Build your own off-grid electricity: solar panels, a wind turbine or a water wheel feed an inverter, and the inverter powers the whole house around it. Add batteries for the night. The Electrical skill finally matters: every device has a level, and your level makes the installation better.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/img/diagram_en.png[/img]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Sources[/b] produce: solar panel 100 W, wind turbine 400 W, water wheel 120 W. As many as you want per inverter.
description=[*][b]The inverter[/b] (1,500 W max) powers every appliance within 20 tiles and 3 floors up or down. No wiring per appliance: fridges, lights, stoves, TVs and radios work as in vanilla.
description=[*][b]The power bank[/b] stores the surplus: it counts 4 batteries (car batteries or deep-cycle batteries). Without batteries, the house runs on current production only.
description=[*][b]Cables[/b]: electric wire, 1 per 4 tiles, range 12 tiles. Right-click a device > "Live Wire" > "Connect to an inverter".
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Equipment[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/img/equipment_en.png[/img]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Solar panel[/b] (Electrical 3): outdoors, open sky. Flat roofs work.
description=[*][b]Wind turbine[/b] (6): outdoors, stronger higher up.
description=[*][b]Water wheel[/b] (7): on a river bank, ground floor.
description=[*][b]Inverter[/b] (5): the heart of the network, can stand indoors.
description=[*][b]Power bank[/b] (2): opens like a crate.
description=[*][b]Optional[/b]: breaker panel (trips instead of damaging the inverter), low battery alarm, soundproofing kit for the inverter hum, vanilla generator as a backup (started under 20% battery), vanilla desktop computer as management computer.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Production[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/img/production_en.png[/img]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Solar[/b]: about 0.94 kWh per summer day, about 0.60 in winter. Clouds and snow reduce it.
description=[*][b]Wind[/b]: nothing under 10 km/h, rising with the cube of the speed up to 45 km/h, full power from 45 to 85, automatic brake above.
description=[*][b]Water wheel[/b]: 120 W day and night on rivers.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Example: one fridge-freezer and 10 lights use about 4.1 kWh per day. A water wheel and 2 panels cover it all year round.
description=

description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/img/recipe_book_en.png[/img]

description=Category Electrical, learnt automatically at the required level. Solar cells come from dismantled calculators or are found as loot; ready-made equipment can also turn up in electricians', hardware stores and garages.

description=[h2]Management software[/h2]
description=
description=Live Wire Monitor shows production, consumption, batteries, 48 h history and a forecast "until sunrise". With the optional Computer Mod or Laptop Mod it comes on a disk found in computer loot. Otherwise a vanilla desktop computer linked to the inverter runs it.
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/shots/livewire_monitor_without_compute.png[/img]

description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=
description=The server computes everything and re-checks every request. The installation keeps running while players are away: fridges keep food cold. Not yet tested on a dedicated server, so please report any problem.
