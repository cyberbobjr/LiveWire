description=[h1]LiveWire - Electrical Skill Overhaul[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Build 42.21+ · Singleplayer & Multiplayer[/b]
description=
description=The power is out for good, but the lights do not have to be. Build your own off-grid electricity: solar panels, wind turbines and water wheels feed an inverter that powers the house around it, with batteries for the night. Every device has an Electrical level, and your level makes the installation better.

description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=
description=Solar 100 W, wind 400 W, water wheel 120 W. An inverter powers appliances within 20 tiles and ±3 floors, up to 1,500 W, with no cable per appliance. Surplus goes into power banks.
description=While the public grid works, an inverter inside a building also draws up to a fifth of its power from the mains (sandbox option GridCharging, on by default).
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-01-getting-started]Getting started[/url]
description=
description=[h2]Equipment[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/images/diagrams/equipment_en.png[/img]
description=
description=Placing needs a screwdriver, no level. Linking: solar panels and inverters level 3; wind turbines, water wheels and power banks level 2. Optional: breaker, battery alarm, soundproofing, vanilla backup generator, management computer.
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-02-equipment]Equipment guide[/url]
description=
description=[h2]Production[/h2]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/main/docs/images/diagrams/production_en.png[/img]
description=
description=Solar output depends on direction: south 100%, east and west 85%, north 55%. Clouds, snow and dirt reduce it; rain cleans the panels. Wind produces from 10 km/h, at full power from 30 to 85 km/h. The water wheel works day and night.
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-04-production-and-calculations]Production and calculations[/url]
description=
description=[h2]Wiring & coupling[/h2]
description=
description=Right-click a device, then LiveWire, then Connect to an inverter. A cable costs 1 wire per 4 tiles, range 12. Need more than 1,500 W? Couple a second inverter (Couple to an inverter, 1 Inverter Coupling Kit): a network holds 4 inverters, 6,000 W.
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-03-wiring]Wiring guide[/url]
description=
description=[h2]Inverter screen & management software[/h2]
description=
description=The inverter screen switches lighting, equipment and main levers, and shows the watts of each circuit. LiveWire Monitor adds consumers, cable views, a three-day forecast and the last 30 events on a powered management computer.
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-05-inverter-screen-and-software]Inverter screen and software[/url]
description=
description=[h2]Water pump[/h2]
description=
description=A water pump (Electrical 3) feeds plumbed fixtures within 15 tiles, from up to 8 adjacent rain collectors or [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3436537035]Useful Barrels[/url].
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-07-water-pump]Water pump guide[/url]

description=[h2]Electrical skill & safety[/h2]
description=
description=Test batteries at Electrical 1, 3 and 5 for more detail. Live work has a shock risk, on by default: 50% at level 0, 0% at level 6+. Switch off the master inverter first; opening a breaker does not isolate it.
description=[url=https://github.com/cyberbobjr/LiveWire/wiki/EN-10-electrical-skill-and-maintenance]Electrical skill and maintenance[/url]
description=

description=
